Companion to `LANDING-PAGE-COPY.md` (the copy itself) and `HEUSOM-COPY-REFERENCE.md` (the source being cloned). This doc records the strategy: what we cloned, what we changed and why, where Heusom is beatable, the mobile/older-audience research the build follows, and the launch checklist.

## 1. The play in one paragraph

Heusom's Silent Groom Pro page is the proven direct-response playbook for this exact product at this exact price: 40% anchor, tiered bundles, clipper-fear agitation, feature deep-dives, objection-titled testimonials, comparison table, repeated guarantee, long how-to FAQ, free eBook bribe. We clone that skeleton 1:1 — it works — and beat them on four fronts they can't easily follow: a quieter machine framed through a **named mechanism** (the startle reflex / fear memory), the **LED quick-finder light** (hardware they don't have, answering their own best-converting testimonial theme), **guilt absolution** woven through the emotional copy (the strongest documented pet-parent trigger, absent from their page), and **concrete, consistent trust specifics** (1–3 day shipping, 30-day guarantee) where their page leaks credibility.

## 4. Mobile & older-audience build rules (research-backed)

Audience is 25–90, mobile-majority. The Liquid build follows these findings:

- **Sticky bottom buy-bar** (thumb zone, appears when hero ATC scrolls away, ~56px, transform-animated): +5–15% mobile CVR in industry syntheses.
- **Tier picker = full-width tappable cards**, never a dropdown; featured tier pre-selected with badge and border; ≥48px tap targets. Recognition over recall for older users.
- **No accordions / no hidden content anywhere.** NN/g: 65+ users complete web tasks ~43% slower; hidden content adds a tap plus memory load. One checkout A/B showed a 29.1% relative conversion *decrease* from accordion format. FAQ, shipping, and guarantee all render fully open.
- **Type: minimum 16–18px body, near-black on cream/white, high contrast.** Interactive elements generously sized.
- **Long-form copy stays** (copy-heavy converts in high-scrutiny purchases) but formatted for skimming: bolded lead line per paragraph, 1–3 short sentences after it, subhead every few blocks, image/color-block break every 2–4 paragraphs.
- **Few photos**: only AI-generated imagery exists today, so the layout leans on typography, color blocks, icons, and the comparison table. Image slots are optional and schema-controlled for when real photos/UGC arrive.
- **Guarantee visible near price** and repeated mid-page and pre-CTA (badge-with-text pattern: +6–10% typical, up to 32% in case studies — directional).

## 5. Design language

Extracted from the live lullo.us (Shopify Horizon v4.1.0):

- **Type: Fredoka everywhere** (site uses a single-family system; heading 600–800, body 400).
- **Palette:** primary green `#1f6b43`, deep forest `#015704`, body text `#243024`, creams `#eef1ea` / `#ece8dd` / `#f0ece1`, sage `#d2dad2`, olive border `#bebf87`, star gold `#f2a900`.
- **Shapes:** buttons radius 14px, pills 40–99px, cards 16px.
- **Spa evolution (approved):** botanical/leaf accents, airy spacing, sage/eucalyptus tones layered on the green/cream base — "spa day for your pet's paws." Rendered with CSS/SVG motifs (leaf divider, soft radial washes), not photos, so it works with zero real photography. All spa accent colors are schema settings and can be dialed back from the admin.
